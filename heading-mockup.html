Proposed Detection Rules

Rule Condition What it catches
1. Large font (existing) fontSize > bodySize × 1.15 AND length < 100 Title-sized headings
2. Bold font (new) fontName contains "Bold" AND length < 100 Most PDF headings — section titles, Q&A questions
3. ALL CAPS (new) text === text.toUpperCase() AND length 3–80 AND has letters Section headers like "AGREEMENT AND IMPLEMENTATION"
4. Font change + short (new) fontName ≠ bodyFont AND length < 80 AND preceded by gap Italic or alternate-font headings
